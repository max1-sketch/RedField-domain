Welcome to {{SITE_TITLE}}

This is a quick tour of your ticket dashboard — how to customize the bot and how to actually use this day-to-day. Takes about 30 seconds.

🗂️

Set up your panels

Head to Panel Configs to edit what each ticket type says, its color, and which roles get pinged. You can post each one as its own button, or combine them all into a single "Open A Ticket" dropdown — right from that page, no Discord commands required.

🎫

Work tickets live

Open Tickets shows everything in progress. Claim a ticket, reply from the browser, and set its priority — the channel name updates automatically so the rest of the team can see who's on it and how urgent it is at a glance.

🛡️

Moderation & staff tools

Warn, timeout, or fully restrict a staff member's access from Moderation — and every action anyone takes on this dashboard, staff or admin, shows up in the audit Logging tab.

⚙️

Make it yours

Set your reply name and check your permission level any time from My Account. That's the tour — you can always come back to any of this, nothing here is one-time.
